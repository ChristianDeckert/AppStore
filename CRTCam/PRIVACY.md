# Privacy Policy — CRT Retro Cam

**Last updated: 27 November 2026**

## The short version

CRT Retro Cam collects nothing and has no servers of its own. Your photos stay on your device until you
export one yourself. Purchases go through Apple's App Store. There are no ads, no analytics, no
accounts and no tracking.

## The camera

CRT Retro Cam needs the camera to take photos and to measure a screen's flicker. It asks for camera access
the first time you open the camera. The live picture is processed on your device only: when CRT Retro Cam
detects a screen's refresh rate, it reads the brightness of the picture over a few seconds and
keeps nothing of it. Nothing from the camera is recorded or sent anywhere unless you take a photo.

CRT Retro Cam never asks for your location, so its photos carry no location.

## Your photos

Photos you take are kept in CRT Retro Cam's own private storage on your device, together with a small
file per photo that records when it was taken, the camera and lens, the refresh rate and the
shutter settings it used, and whether you marked it as a favourite. They do not go to your photo
library, to iCloud Photos or anywhere else until you export them.

With Premium, you export a photo only by choosing to:

- **Save to Photos** adds it to your photo library. CRT Retro Cam asks for permission to add photos the
  first time; it can only add photos and cannot see your library.
- **AirDrop & share** hands it to the system share sheet, and it goes only where you send it, for
  example via AirDrop, Messages or Mail.

Without Premium, photos cannot leave the app.

Deleting a photo in CRT Retro Cam removes it from the app's storage. Deleting the app removes all of its
photos that you have not exported.

## What else CRT Retro Cam stores on your device

- **Preferences:** the refresh rate to start with, whether the controls are folded, the viewfinder
  grid and the other switches in Settings, and whether you have seen the guide.
- **A technical log:** each session writes a log of what the camera did (the camera and format
  used, exposures, refresh rates measured, errors), used to fix problems. It contains no pictures
  and no personal information, stays on your device, and only the newest 20 sessions are kept.
- **Premium status:** whether Premium is active, derived from the purchase information Apple's
  StoreKit provides on your device.

If iCloud Backup is on, this data may be included in your own device backup, which only you can
reach. It is never sent to us.

## Network

CRT Retro Cam's only network connection is for in-app purchases. Buying Premium, restoring it and
checking whether it is active go through Apple's App Store with StoreKit, Apple's purchase
framework. Apple processes the payment and keeps the purchase record under your Apple Account;
CRT Retro Cam receives no payment details, name or e-mail address, only whether Premium is active.
Apple's privacy policy applies: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

CRT Retro Cam has no analytics, no crash reporting of its own, no advertising and no update checks.
Everything else works offline.

## Android

On Android, CRT Retro Cam works the same way: it uses the camera, keeps your photos in its own storage
until you export them, and has no network connection. Premium is not available on Android yet;
when it is, this policy will describe how purchases work there.

## Data collection

None. CRT Retro Cam has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does
not share data with data brokers. Purchase records are held by Apple; the app stores only whether
Premium is active. Its App Store privacy declaration is "Data Not Collected".

## Children

CRT Retro Cam collects nothing from anyone, of any age.
