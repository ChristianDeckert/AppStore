# Analog Clock — Support

Analog Clock turns an iPhone, iPad or Apple TV into a full-screen analog clock, with designs from
classic watches to playful scenes for kids. This page covers how to use it, common questions, and
how to reach us.

## Getting started on iPhone and iPad

The app has three tabs.

- **Clock** shows the design full screen. Tap the clock to show the controls; they fade away again
  after a few seconds. Tap the weather to open the hourly forecast.
- **Gallery** lists every design, grouped into Timepieces, Pop, Playful, Videos and Legacy, plus
  the designs you saved yourself under **My Designs**. Tap one and the clock shows it. Touch and
  hold a design to customize it; your own designs can also be deleted there.
- **Studio** changes the design on screen: dial, markers, numerals, hands, complications,
  background, shapes, weather and day & night. Tap **Save** to keep the result under My Designs,
  or **Revert** to undo your changes.

## Getting started on Apple TV

- The **Clock** tab shows the design. Swipe down into the clock and the tab bar hides, so only the
  clock is on screen. Press **Back** (or **Menu** on older remotes) to bring the tab bar back.
- Press **Select** on the clock for the hourly forecast, and **Back** to close it.
- The **Gallery** tab lists every design. Select one and the clock shows it.

Designs are created and changed in the iPhone and iPad app; Apple TV only picks from the
included designs.

## Weather

- Allow location access when the app asks. The weather then appears on the dial or in the bar at
  the top, depending on the design, usually within a minute.
- In the Studio's **Weather** section, **Show weather** turns it on or off for a design, and
  **Position** chooses between the dial and the bar at the top.
- The weather comes from Apple Weather. If that service is not available, the app uses
  OpenWeather instead.

## Day and night

- Under **Studio → Day & night**, switch on **Night time enabled** and choose when day and night
  start. At night the face dims and can show its own background colour or image. A morning sound
  can greet the new day.
- The **Screensaver** switch under **Studio → Other options** blanks the screen for 10 seconds
  every 16 minutes, to spare displays that show the clock all day. This is intended, and the clock
  returns by itself.

## The app icon

The app icon changes to match the design you pick. A few seconds after a pick, the system shows
the notice "You have changed the icon"; tap **OK**, or press **Select** on Apple TV. Your own
designs use the standard Aviator icon.

## Troubleshooting

- **The weather does not appear.** Check that location access is allowed for Analog Clock under
  Location Services in the Settings app's privacy settings, and that the design shows the weather.
  A network connection is needed.
- **A video background stays black.** Designs with a YouTube video load it from the internet;
  check your connection. The two video designs on Apple TV are built in and play offline.
- **The icon notice keeps appearing.** It appears once per change of design. Your own designs all
  share one icon, so switching between them shows no notice.
- **My designs from Apple TV are gone.** Designs made on Apple TV in versions before 2.0 could not
  be carried over. Create designs in the iPhone or iPad app instead.
- **The clock looks dim.** Night time may be on: check the times under Studio → Day & night.

## Privacy

Analog Clock has no account, no analytics and no advertising. The weather needs your approximate
location, and YouTube video designs load from YouTube. Full details: [Privacy policy](PRIVACY.md).

## Requirements

Analog Clock requires iOS 17, iPadOS 17 or tvOS 17 or later. The iPhone and iPad app and the
Apple TV app are one purchase.

the design you were using.
