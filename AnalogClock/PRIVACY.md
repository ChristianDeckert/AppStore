# Privacy Policy — Analog Clock

**Last updated: 4 November 2026**

## The short version

Analog Clock has no servers of its own, no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
To show the weather it sends your approximate location to a weather service. On iPhone and iPad,
designs with a YouTube video load that video from YouTube.

## Location and weather

If you allow it, Analog Clock asks for your location while the app is in use, at an accuracy of
about one kilometre. It uses the location only to request the current weather and the hourly
forecast, and does not store it.

- The request goes to **Apple Weather** (WeatherKit), Apple's weather service. Apple's privacy
  policy applies: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
- If Apple Weather is not available, the app asks **OpenWeather** instead and sends it the
  coordinates and your language setting. OpenWeather's privacy policy applies:
  https://openweather.co.uk/privacy-policy

The latest weather result is kept on your device so the clock can show it right away at the next
launch. If you do not allow location access, no location is sent anywhere and the weather simply
stays hidden. You can change the permission at any time in the Settings app.

## YouTube videos (iPhone and iPad)

Some designs, and designs you make yourself, can show a YouTube video as background. The Studio
also has a YouTube browser for choosing one. Only when you use these does the app connect to
YouTube, which is operated by Google. YouTube may set cookies, kept in the app's own web storage on
your device, and Google's privacy policy applies: https://policies.google.com/privacy

The Apple TV app does not connect to YouTube; its two video designs are built into the app.

## Photos

When you add a photo to a design, you choose it with the system photo picker. The app receives only
the photo you pick, not access to your photo library, and stores a copy with the design on your
device.

## Your designs and settings

Your designs, the photos in them, and your settings — such as the design on screen and the day and
night hours — are stored only on your device, in the app's own storage. They are not synced and
never sent to us. If iCloud Backup is on, they may be included in your own backup, which only you
can reach.

## Data collection

We do not collect any data. Analog Clock has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no crash
reporting of its own, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not share data with data brokers.
The only data that leaves your device are the requests described above, sent directly to Apple
Weather, OpenWeather or YouTube.

## Children

Analog Clock includes designs made for children. It collects nothing from anyone, of any age.
