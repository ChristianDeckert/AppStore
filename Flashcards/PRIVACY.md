# Privacy Policy — Flashcards

**Last updated: 19 November 2026**

## The short version

Flashcards has no servers of its own and we collect nothing. Your piles stay on your device and in
your own iCloud. When you ask for a translation, the text you want translated is sent to Google's
translation service. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

## Your piles and cards

Your piles, cards and training progress are stored on your device, in the app's own storage. If
iCloud Backup is on, they may be included in your own backup, which only you can reach.

## iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, Flashcards syncs your piles and cards between your devices through
Apple's iCloud, in your private iCloud database. Only you can reach this data; we cannot see it.
Apple's privacy policy applies: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

You can turn iCloud off for Flashcards in the Settings app at any time; your piles then stay only on
the device.

## Translation

When you tap a language while writing a card, Flashcards sends the text on the card's front and the
chosen language to Google's translation service (translate.googleapis.com) and shows the result.
Like any internet request, this includes your device's IP address. Nothing is sent unless you tap a
language, and no account or identifier of yours is sent. Google's privacy policy applies:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Sharing piles

When you share a pile, Flashcards creates a file with the pile's name, color and cards and hands it
to the share sheet. It goes only where you send it, for example via AirDrop, Messages or Mail. When
you open a pile someone shared with you, it is added to your piles only after you tap **Add to my
piles**, and the received file is deleted afterwards.

## In-app purchase

Flashcards offers one optional purchase, "Premium", which unlocks unlimited piles and cards. It is
sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. Flashcards never sees your payment details; it
stores only whether the purchase is active, on your device.

## Data collection

We do not collect any data. Flashcards has no account of its own, no analytics, no crash reporting
of its own, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not share data with data brokers. The only
data that leaves your device is described above: your piles in your own iCloud, text you ask to have
translated, and piles you choose to share.

## Children

Flashcards collects nothing from anyone, of any age. Translation sends only the text you choose to
translate, as described above.
