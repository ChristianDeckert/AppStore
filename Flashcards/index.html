Flashcards for anything.

Learn vocabulary, capitals, facts, anything, with piles of colorful cards you write yourself. Flip, say the answer out loud, and train a pile in minutes.

Free for 5 piles of 10 cards. Premium is a one-time purchase.
Requires iOS or iPadOS 17 or later.
On iPhone

A closer look

How it works

Write it, flip it, know it

Write your own piles

Start a pile for anything you want to learn and give it a color. Write the front, flip the card, write the back, and tap Save, next card for the next blank one.

Translation built in

Tap a language above the card, for example ES, and the front is translated into the back for you, in thirteen languages. You can always edit the result.

Train in minutes

Flip, then Got it or Again. Missed cards come back in the same run. The swap button shows the backs first, for training in the other direction.

Pick up where you left off

The home screen shows the pile you worked on last. Example piles in your language, such as animals or capitals, get you started right away.

Synced with iCloud

Signed in to iCloud, your piles sync between iPhone and iPad by themselves. The ⋯ menu shows the sync status.

Share a pile

Send a pile via AirDrop, Messages, Mail or Files. Your friends open it and tap Add to my piles to get their own copy.

New in 2.1

Fix a typo in seconds: hold a card to edit or delete it. While writing, Return flips the card, and Return again saves it.

On iPad

Made for iPad

On iPad, your piles and the open pile sit next to each other. In Split View or a small Stage Manager window, Flashcards switches to the same layout as on iPhone.

Free and Premium

Free for 5 piles of 10 cards

Premium is a one-time purchase that unlocks unlimited piles and cards. There is no subscription, and it is shared on all your devices with the same Apple Account. Example piles don't count towards the free limit.

Privacy

No account, no analytics, no ads

Your piles stay on your device and in your own iCloud. When you ask for a translation, the text you want translated is sent to Google's translation service. Read the privacy policy.
