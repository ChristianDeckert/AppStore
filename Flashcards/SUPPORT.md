# Flashcards — Support

Flashcards helps you learn anything with piles of colorful cards you write yourself: a word or
question on the front, its meaning or answer on the back. This page covers how the app works,
common questions, and how to reach us.

## Piles and cards

- Tap the **+** button on the home screen to start a pile. Give it a name and a color.
- Open a pile and tap **+** to write cards. Write the front, flip the card, write the back, and tap
  **Save, next card** for the next blank card.
- Tap a card in a pile to flip it. Tap **Edit** to remove cards, or **Edit** on the home screen to
  remove piles.
- The palette button in a pile changes its color.
- The home screen shows the pile you worked on last under **Pick up where you left off**.

## Translation

While writing a card, tap a language above it, for example **ES**, and the front is translated into
the back for you. Thirteen languages are available. Translation needs an internet connection; you
can always edit the result or write the back yourself.

## Training

- Tap **Start training** in a pile. Read the card, say the answer out loud, then tap **Flip**.
- Tap **Got it** if you knew it, or **Again** to see the card once more later in the same run.
- The swap button at the top shows the backs first, for training in the other direction.
- When every card is known, the pile is cleared and you see your time and retries. Tap
  **Shuffle and go again** for another run.

## Example piles

On first launch, Flashcards adds example piles in your language, such as animals or capitals, so
you can try training right away. They don't count towards the free limit. Delete the ones you
don't need; **Reset app…** in the **⋯** menu brings them back.

## iCloud sync

When you are signed in to iCloud, your piles sync between your iPhone and iPad automatically. The
**⋯** menu on the home screen shows the sync status. Piles you had before updating are uploaded by
themselves.

## Sharing piles

Open a pile and tap the share button to send it as a `.flashcards` file via AirDrop, Messages, Mail
or Files. The person receiving it opens the file in Flashcards and taps **Add to my piles**; they
get their own copy. In the Files app, tap the file, then the share button, then **Flashcards**.

## iPad

On iPad, your piles and the open pile sit side by side. In Split View or a small Stage Manager
window, Flashcards switches to the same layout as on iPhone.

## Premium

Flashcards is free for up to 5 piles with 10 cards each. **Premium** is a one-time purchase that
unlocks unlimited piles and cards; there is no subscription. It is shared on all your devices with
the same Apple Account. On a new device or after reinstalling, open Premium and tap **Restore
purchase**.

## Reset

**Reset app…** in the **⋯** menu deletes all piles and cards and brings back the example piles, like
after a fresh install. With iCloud sync on, the piles are deleted on your other devices too. Your
Premium purchase is kept.

## Troubleshooting

- **My piles don't appear on my other device.** Check that both devices are signed in to the same
  Apple Account and that Flashcards is allowed to use iCloud in Settings › [your name] › iCloud,
  among the apps using iCloud. Syncing can
  take a few minutes, and the app needs to be opened on both devices.
- **The ⋯ menu says "iCloud is off".** Sign in to iCloud in the Settings app. Your piles stay safe
  on the device and upload once you sign in.
- **Translation does not work.** Check your internet connection and try again.
- **A shared pile opens as text.** Some apps show the file's contents instead of opening it. Tap the
  share button and choose **Flashcards**.
- **"This pile was made with a newer version of Flashcards."** Update the app from the App Store.
- **My purchase is not active.** Open Premium and tap **Restore purchase**, using the Apple Account
  you bought it with.
