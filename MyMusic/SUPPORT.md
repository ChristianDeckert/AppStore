# My Music — Support

My Music plays the music files you own, on iPhone and iPad, and the sound of your videos. Your
library is a folder of real folders that you fill from your computer or the Files app. There is no
streaming and no account. This page covers how the app works, common questions, and how to reach
us.

## Getting music in

- **From your computer.** Connect your iPhone or iPad with a cable and select it in the Finder
  sidebar on a Mac, or in the Apple Devices app on Windows. Open the **Files** tab and drag songs or
  whole folders onto **My Music**. Folders keep their structure.
- **From the Files app.** On the Library screen, tap **+ › Import files**; inside a folder, tap
  **⋯ › Import files**. You can pick many files at once, or whole folders from iCloud Drive or any
  other location in Files, and their subfolders come along. Only audio and video files and cover
  images (such as `cover.jpg`) are copied; anything else is skipped, and the message afterwards says
  how many. If a name is already taken, the copy gets a number, for example "Live Set 2".
- **From other apps.** In Mail, Files, Safari downloads or another app, share an audio or video
  file and choose **My Music**. It is copied to the top of your library.

My Music's folder also appears in the Files app under **On My iPhone › My Music** (or On My iPad).
Anything you add, rename or move there shows up in the app by itself.

On first launch with an empty library, My Music adds one demo song so you can try the player right
away. Delete it whenever you like.

## Free and Premium

- My Music is free for up to **25 files** in your library, with every feature included. Every song
  and every video counts, in whichever folder it sits; cover images and playlists don't.
- Once your library holds 25 files, adding more through the app (**Import files**, opening a file
  in My Music from another app, or sharing one to it) asks you to unlock **Premium**. Premium is a
  one-time in-app purchase that removes the limit for good. There is no subscription.
- Without Premium, nothing is hidden or locked: all your files stay visible and playable. Files you
  copy in through Finder or the Files app still appear, and a card in the library invites you to
  upgrade. Deleting files makes room again.
- **⋯ › Settings › Premium** shows whether Premium is active and how many of the 25 files you use,
  with **Unlock** and **Restore purchases**.
- Purchases are Apple in-app purchases, handled by the App Store; My Music has no account of its
  own. Premium is the same purchase as in My Music 1.x: if you bought it before, on this or another
  device, it comes back through your Apple Account. If Settings doesn't show it as active, tap
  **Restore purchases**.
- Refunds are handled by Apple at https://reportaproblem.apple.com.

## Folders, albums and playlists

- **Folders are real folders.** The folders in My Music are the folders on your device, the same
  ones Finder and the Files app show. Create one with **+ › New folder**, or don't: a flat library
  works fine, and search and the collections reach every song.
- **Albums are detected automatically.** A folder with two or more songs that all share one album
  tag, and no subfolders with songs, is shown as an album and sorted by track number.
- **Playlists are the only virtual grouping.** A playlist points to songs wherever they are; it does
  not copy or move files. Create one with **+ › New playlist** on the Library screen, or from a
  song's menu under **Add to playlist › New playlist…**. To turn a folder into a playlist, long-press
  it and choose **Make playlist from folder**. Open a playlist to reorder or remove its songs.
- **Collections.** The Library screen shows **Recently played**, and the collections
  **Favourites**, **Most played**, **Recently added** and **Music videos**.
- **Sorting and layout.** Sort a folder by title, artist, album, track number or date added, and
  switch between list and grid. Both are remembered per folder.

## Playing music

- Tap a song to play it; the rest of its folder or playlist follows. **Play** and **Shuffle** at the
  top of a folder play everything in it, subfolders included.
- The **mini player** sits at the bottom. Swipe it left or right to skip; tap it or swipe up to open
  the full player; swipe down on the full player to close it.
- **Scrub ring:** drag the handle around the round cover, like a clock hand, to move through the
  song. It works while paused too.
- **Shuffle and repeat** (all or one song) are in the full player and in Up Next.
- **Up Next** shows what plays after the current song. Drag to reorder, swipe to remove. Add songs
  with **Play next** or **Play later** from a song's menu.
- **Sleep timer:** **⋯** in the full player › **Sleep timer**: 15, 30, 45 or 60 minutes, or the end
  of the current song.
- **Visualizers:** choose **Bars ring**, **Spectrum**, **Wave** or off with the visualizer button in
  the full player. They react to the music and take their colours from the cover. With Reduce
  Motion turned on, they move more calmly.
- **AirPlay:** use the route button in the full player.
- **Lock screen and Control Center** show the cover and position, and you can scrub, pause and skip
  from there.
- My Music remembers the song and position when you close it and picks up there next time. Songs in
  the same audio format play back to back without a gap.

## Video files

My Music adds MP4, M4V and MOV videos to your library and plays them as audio, like any song: you
hear the sound, there is no video picture. That makes a concert or festival set that only exists as
a video easy to listen to. A video's cover is its embedded artwork or, if it has none, a frame from
the video. The **Music videos** collection on the Library screen lists all your videos.

## Long-press menus

Long-press a song, folder or playlist to open its menu.

- **Song:** Play next, Play later, Share…, Add to playlist, Favourite, Move to ‹last folder›, Move…,
  Edit details, Update artwork… (MP3 and M4A only), Remove from playlist (inside a playlist),
  Delete….
- **Folder:** Play, Shuffle, Share…, Move to ‹last folder›, Move…, Rename…, Make playlist from
  folder, Update artwork…, Delete….
- **Playlist:** Play, Share…, Rename…, Remove missing songs (when there are any), Delete playlist….

**Move to ‹last folder›** repeats your last move in one tap, and every move can be undone from the
message that follows. **Share…** opens the share sheet for AirDrop, Messages, Mail or Save to Files;
a folder is sent as a zip file. **Delete…** asks first: deleting a song or folder removes the files
from your device, while deleting a playlist keeps its songs in your library.

## Editing details

**Edit details** changes the title, artist, album, track number and cover. My Music writes them
into the file itself, so Finder, the Music app and other players see the same details.

- **MP3** files get ID3 tags; **M4A** and **M4B** files get iTunes-style metadata.
- **FLAC, WAV, AIFF, CAF and AAC** files are shown read-only.
- When My Music saves an MP3, uncommon ID3 frames that it does not edit may not be kept. This
  includes custom text frames (TXXX) such as ReplayGain values, private frames (PRIV) and ratings
  (POPM). If those matter to you, keep a copy of the original file.

## Covers

My Music shows the cover embedded in a song. If there is none, it uses a `cover.jpg`,
`folder.jpg` or `front.jpg` (or `.png`) in the same folder.

To change a cover, long-press a song or folder and choose **Update artwork…**:

- My Music searches Apple's iTunes catalogue right away, for the song's artist and album or the
  folder's name. Edit the search field if needed. Tap a result to preview it, then tap **Use**.
- Or tap **Photos** or **Files** and pick any image.
- For a single song, **Apply to all songs in ‹folder›** gives the whole folder the same cover. On a
  folder, the cover goes to every song in it.

The cover is saved into the MP3 and M4A files. The cover search needs an internet connection; the
rest of My Music works offline.

## Search

Tap the search button on the Library screen, or pull down on any list. Pulling down inside a folder
searches only that folder. Results are grouped into songs, folders and playlists, and the chips
**All**, **Songs**, **Folders** and **Playlists** narrow them down. Your recent searches appear
when the field is empty.

## iPad

On iPad, your library sits in a column on the left and the player stays open on the right. In
landscape, Up Next appears as a panel beside the player. In Split View, Slide Over or a narrow
Stage Manager window, My Music switches to the same layout as on iPhone.

## Settings

Open **⋯ › Settings** on the Library screen.

- **Premium** shows whether Premium is active and how many of the 25 free files you use, with
  **Unlock** and **Restore purchases**. See [Free and Premium](#free-and-premium).
- **Adding music** repeats the three ways to get songs in.
- **Rescan library** reads all folders again. You rarely need it: My Music watches your folders and
  scans each time you return to the app. It is also in each folder's **⋯** menu.
- **Clear recent searches** empties the search history.
- **Clear listening history** resets play counts and Recently played. Your songs, playlists and
  favourites are not affected.

## Supported formats

- **Audio:** MP3, M4A (including Apple Lossless), M4B, AAC, WAV, AIFF (`.aif`, `.aiff`, `.aifc`),
  CAF and FLAC.
- **Video, played as audio:** MP4, M4V and MOV.

Files in other formats, such as OGG, WMA or the YouTube and Vimeo bookmarks (`.ytbm`, `.vbm`) from
My Music 1.x, stay in the folder but don't appear in the app.

## Troubleshooting

- **My songs don't appear.** Check that they are in one of the formats above. If you copied them
  from a computer, wait until the copy has finished, then choose **⋯ › Rescan library**.
- **I moved or renamed files in Finder or the Files app.** My Music recognises a moved file by its
  size and modification date and keeps its favourite, play count and playlist entries. A file that
  was also changed on the way, for example re-tagged on a computer, counts as a new song.
- **A playlist says "N missing".** Some of its songs were deleted or can no longer be found. The
  playlist keeps them in case they come back, for example when you move a file back. To clean up,
  long-press the playlist and choose **Remove missing songs**.
- **"Your playlists and favourites file was damaged."** My Music keeps a copy of that file before
  every change. It found the latest version unreadable and restored the previous one, so your most
  recent changes to playlists or favourites may be missing.
- **My Music asks me to unlock Premium when I add files.** Your library holds 25 songs and videos,
  the free limit. Unlock Premium, or delete files you no longer need; see
  [Free and Premium](#free-and-premium).
- **I bought Premium, but My Music still asks me to unlock it.** Make sure you are signed in to the
  App Store with the Apple Account you bought it with, then tap **Restore purchases** in
  **⋯ › Settings › Premium**.
- **I can't edit a song's details.** Only MP3, M4A and M4B files can be edited; other formats are
  shown read-only.
- **The cover search finds nothing.** Check your internet connection and try a shorter search, such
  as the artist and album name. You can always pick an image from Photos or Files instead.
- **I'm coming from My Music 1.x.** Your music files and folders are all still there. If you bought
  Premium, it comes back through your Apple Account; otherwise tap **Restore purchases** in
  **⋯ › Settings › Premium**. Videos now play as audio, without a picture. YouTube and Vimeo
  bookmarks (`.ytbm`, `.vbm`) are not supported and don't appear in the app; their files stay in the
  My Music folder in the Files app.

## Privacy

My Music has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Its only network
connections are the cover search, which sends your search term to Apple's iTunes Search service,
and in-app purchases, which go through Apple's App Store. Full details: [Privacy policy](PRIVACY.md).

## Requirements

My Music requires iOS 18 or iPadOS 18 or later. It is available in English and German.
