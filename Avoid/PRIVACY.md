# Privacy Policy — Avoid

**Last updated: 4 November 2026**

## The short version

Avoid collects nothing and has no servers of its own. Scores and achievements go to Apple's Game
Center, and the one optional purchase goes through Apple's App Store. There are no ads, no
analytics and no tracking.

## Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, Avoid sends your best times to its two leaderboards and
reports your progress on its survival achievements. These are handled by Apple, and the
leaderboards are visible to other Game Center players as your Game Center settings allow. Apple's
privacy policy applies: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

If you are not signed in, nothing is sent. Times from games played offline wait on your device and
are submitted the next time you sign in. You can sign in or out of Game Center at any time in the
Settings app.

## In-app purchase

Avoid offers one optional purchase, "Invincible start", which starts every game with a shield. It
is sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. Avoid never sees your payment details; it
stores only whether the purchase is active, on your device.

## Links

The credits list the artists of the soundtrack, and the "i" button in the menu leads to the
developer's website. Tapping one of these opens the address in your web browser; Avoid itself
does not connect to it.

## Data on your device

Your best time for each difficulty, Game Center times waiting to be sent, achievement progress,
and your settings — music, haptics and the last track played — are stored only on your device, in the app's own storage. If iCloud Backup is on, they may be included in your own
backup, which only you can reach. They are never sent to us.

## Data collection

We do not collect any data. Avoid has no account of its own, no analytics, no crash reporting of
its own, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not share data with data brokers. The only
data that leaves your device is described above and goes directly to Apple.

## Children

Avoid collects nothing from anyone, of any age.
