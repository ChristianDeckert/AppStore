# Avoid — Support

Avoid is a one-tap arcade game: blocks fall down a neon lane, and you switch walls to dodge them for
as long as you can. This page covers how to play, common questions, and how to reach us.

## How to play

- Tap anywhere on the title to start. Tap the difficulty row below it to switch between **easy** and
  **hard**.
- Tap to move your player to the other wall and out of the way of falling blocks.
- **Easy** has one lane. **Hard** has two lanes side by side, one for each thumb.
- The game gets faster and the patterns get denser the longer you survive. Your score is the time
  you last, in seconds.
- Dodging a block at the very last moment is a **near miss** and earns extra time.

## Power-ups

Glowing circles fall between the blocks. Catch one with your player to use it. They give you, for
example, slow motion, a shield that absorbs one hit, or a **shockwave**: shockwave charges collect
on the red fire button, and firing one clears the whole field.

## Invincible start

The shop in the menu sells one optional upgrade, **Invincible start**: every game begins with a
shield that absorbs your first hit. Buy it once and keep it on all your devices with the same
Apple Account. On a new device or after reinstalling, open the shop and tap **Restore Purchases**.

## Game Center

Sign in to Game Center to compete on the easy and hard leaderboards and to earn the ten survival
achievements, from 15 to 150 seconds on hard. Tap **Game Center** in the menu to see them. If the
app shows "Sign in to Game Center in Settings", sign in under Game Center in the Settings app.

## Pausing

The game pauses when a call comes in or you switch to another app, and continues with a 3-2-1
countdown when you return. Tap to skip the countdown.

## Sound and haptics

The note button in the menu turns the music on and off. On iPhone, the button above it turns haptic
feedback on and off. If you are already playing music from another app, Avoid leaves it playing and
keeps its own soundtrack off.

## Troubleshooting

- **My purchase is not active.** Open the shop and tap **Restore Purchases**, using the Apple
  Account you bought it with. If the purchase is waiting for approval (Ask to Buy), it activates by
  itself once approved.
- **My scores do not appear on the leaderboard.** Check that you are signed in to Game Center.
  Times from offline games are sent the next time you sign in.
- **There is no music.** Check the note button in the menu, the ring/silent switch, and whether
  another app is playing audio.
- **I lost my best time after reinstalling.** Sign in to Game Center: Avoid restores your best times
  from your leaderboard entries.

## Privacy

Avoid has no account, no analytics and no advertising. Full details: [Privacy policy](PRIVACY.md).

## Requirements

Avoid requires iOS 17 or iPadOS 17 or later.
