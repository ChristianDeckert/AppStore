Avoid

One tap. Two walls. Endless blocks.

A fast arcade game about survival: blocks rain down a neon synthwave lane, and you switch walls to dodge them for as long as you can. Easy to learn, hard to put down.

Coming soon to the App Store No ads. No account. One optional upgrade.
Requires iOS or iPadOS 17 or later.
One tap to dodge

Tap. You're on the other wall.

Tap anywhere to jump to the other wall and out of the way of the falling blocks. Hold to wait in the middle of the lane, and drag to choose which wall you land on.

Dodge a block at the very last moment for a near miss: it earns you extra time.

Two lanes, one brain

Hard mode splits the screen

Easy has one lane. Hard has two lanes side by side, one for each thumb. Test your multitasking, or hand one lane to a friend and survive together.

It only gets faster

Your score is the time you last

Blocks fall faster and the patterns get denser the longer you survive, and every run is a new random field. Tap the title to start, and the row below it to switch between easy and hard.

More

Neon, power-ups and a leaderboard

Power-ups

Glowing circles fall between the blocks. Catch one with your player to use it, plus a few that turn the game upside down.

  • Slow motion
  • Shield
  • Shockwave

Shockwave charges collect on the red fire button. Firing one clears the whole field.

A synthwave world

Glowing lanes, a striped retro sun and a city skyline that pulses to 13 chiptune and synthwave tracks by independent artists.

Compete

Game Center leaderboards for easy and hard, and ten survival achievements, from 15 to 150 seconds on hard. Sign in to Game Center and your best times come back after a reinstall.

Smooth and responsive

Runs at your display's full refresh rate, up to 120 Hz on ProMotion, with haptic feedback on iPhone. After a call, a 3-2-1 countdown brings you right back.

The one upgrade

Invincible start

The shop in the menu sells one optional upgrade: every game begins with a shield that absorbs your first hit. Buy it once and keep it on all your devices with the same Apple Account.

iPad

Bigger lanes, same reflexes

Privacy

No account, no analytics, no ads

Avoid collects nothing and has no servers of its own. Scores and achievements go to Apple's Game Center only if you are signed in, and the one optional purchase goes through Apple's App Store. Read the privacy policy.
