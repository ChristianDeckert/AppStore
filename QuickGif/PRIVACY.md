# Privacy Policy — Quick Gif

**Last updated: 4 November 2026**

## The short version

Quick Gif collects nothing and has no servers of its own. Conversion happens on your Mac.
Purchases go through Apple's App Store.

## What Quick Gif does with your files

Quick Gif converts video and image files that you drop on it, or choose through its open panel,
into animated GIFs. All of that work happens on your Mac.

While a conversion is running, the app writes temporary files to a private folder inside its own
sandboxed container. Those files are deleted when you close the preview window, or at the next
launch if the app quits unexpectedly. The finished GIF is written to your Downloads folder, or
to a folder you choose in Settings.

Quick Gif reads a file only when you give it one. It has no access to the rest of your disk.

## Network

Quick Gif's only network connection is to Apple's App Store, for in-app purchases, handled by
StoreKit. It has no analytics, no crash reporting, no telemetry, no advertising and no update
checks. The bundled conversion tool is compiled without network support of its own.

The one other outbound path is the **FFmpeg source code** link in Settings → About →
Acknowledgements. Following it opens that address in your web browser; Quick Gif itself does
not connect to it.

## Data collection

None. Quick Gif has no account, no sign-in, and no identifier that leaves your Mac. Purchase
records are held by Apple; the app stores only a local credit balance, whether the lifetime
unlock is active, and a local install identifier used to protect that balance. Its App Store
privacy declaration is "Data Not Collected".

## Settings

Your preferences — output folder, frame rate, output size, palette settings, appearance, hot
corner, and credit balance — are stored locally on your Mac, in the app's own preferences. If
macOS iCloud backup is enabled for your Mac, they may be included in your own backup, which
only you can reach. They are never sent to us, because there is no "us" to send them to.

## Children

Quick Gif is rated 4+ and collects nothing from anyone, of any age.
